📌 Overview: what this report can do

Statistical data created from Lunatic7 clear runs of Touhou Kouyouya (LBOL).
It may help you get a rough grasp of the overall flow.

🗳️ Reading the Card Election tab (Test feature β)

Choices between two cards offered at the same time ("A or B") are treated as an election (head-to-head matchup), and a P-rate and stacking metrics are combined into a P-score for each card.
Clicking a card in the scatter plot or card table shows detailed info (draft win rate, Top 5 opponents it lost to, card performance).

* This is a test feature (β). Each character is estimated independently using only that character's L7 TrueEnd runs, so numbers cannot be compared directly between characters. Starting cards (24 Basic cards) are excluded.

Glossary

P-rate
A rating obtained by treating card picks (drafts) as "matchups between the 2 cards offered in the same cell" and applying an Elo rating system.
Of the two cards offered together, the one actually picked is the winner and the other the loser; all cells across all runs are compiled into round-robin match results. An Elo-style rating ("beating a stronger opponent counts more") is applied to those results to score each card (mean = 1000).
Cells where another card was picked, or where the player passed, are not counted. Only cards offered simultaneously 10 or more times are rated.
Example: if A is always picked while B is almost always discarded, A's rate rises a lot. Conversely, losing to weak opponents won't lower the rate too much.
Duels
The denominator of the Draft win rate (decided pair count).
Draft win rate
Win rate at cells where "A or B was picked" (decisions only). The unit is pairs, not cells — a cell with 3+ cards yields multiple pairs (picking in a 10-card shop = 9 duels). Cells where another card was chosen or the player passed are not counted.
Pick rate
Shown in the detail panel. The share of offering cells where this card was picked (passes included = card vs everything else).
Differs from the Draft win rate (decided matchups only) in its denominator. Cards often passed over have a high win rate but a lower pick rate.
Offering flow
Shown in the detail panel. Pick rates when the card was offered for the 1st / 2nd / 3rd+ time in a run (not-owned / owned separately).
Shows whether players who skipped the 1st offer pick it on the 2nd, and whether players who picked it stack more copies later. 1st offer × not-owned = the First Pick Rate.
Selectable offerings
Offerings where the card was actually selectable (in shops: bought or affordable with the displayed money). Pick rate = the share picked among them (passes included).
Real adoption
The share of runs in which the card was acquired at least once (Cards_log Add base) — an effective acquisition rate. Includes cards obtained outside drafts (events, trades, etc.) and cards that vanish upon use (e.g. Devour History).
Final adoption
The share still in the deck at the end of the run (same definition as the "Adoption rate" in the Card List tab).
The difference from Real adoption = cards acquired but used up, vanished, or removed.
P-score
P-rate ÷ 1000 × (1 + 2+ copies rate ÷ 100).
Cards that are both strong and stacked in multiple copies score highest. Strong cards used as a single copy have a high P-rate but a moderate score.
Avg. copies
Average number of copies in runs that acquired the card
2+ copies rate
Share of runs with 2 or more copies (higher for cards with a "stacking" culture)
Pick rate by Act
Not-owned pick rate per Act (* = pick rate when already owned). Cell n (= picks / offerings) is the sample size. Shows how picks change as the run progresses.
Selectable offerings
Out of the offering count, how many were actually selectable under the same rule as the Card List tab (in shops: bought or affordable with the displayed money).
The offering count is shelf-based (for the P-rate), so the difference = unaffordable shop offerings.

🩺 Reading the Similar Run Search tab (Test feature β)

Search for runs with deck compositions similar to yours and view their stats, route tendencies, reinforcement candidate cards and a list of similar runs. You can also jump in from the 🔍 button in the Run Finder results.

Conditions

Deck composition
Add cards to build the search condition (with copy counts). "Load starter deck" starts from that character's starter deck.
📌 Pinning
Pressing 📌 on a card makes it a required condition. Only runs owning at least the number of copies set in your deck qualify (e.g. pin a card ×2 → only runs stacking 2+). Exhibits qualify by ownership.
Mana filter
Restrict to runs whose ending base mana satisfies the condition (e.g. blue 3+).
Exclusion
Starter cards can be excluded from the similarity comparison (pinned starters are not excluded).
Sharing
"🔗 Copy share link" gives a URL with the condition (also kept in search history).

❓ Reading the Event Analysis tab

Choices made at each event are listed in chronological order. Choice labels became more informative as of 2026-10-27.

About the choice labels

Real names
Chosen cards / exhibits are shown with their in-game names.
Event-specific branches (character IDs for "Debut", pill assignments, etc.) are also aggregated per real name.
Mana color events
Mana choices such as 純化光玉 / 光玉精製 are shown with emoji icons: white🟡 blue🔵 black🟣 red🔴 green🟢 colorless🔘.
Hovering shows the color name in the title.
Numbers shown as (…)
Numbers inside event text vary from run to run, so variable parts are collapsed to … (static text of the event itself is kept as-is).
Top-10 block
For each choice it shows the top items that tend to happen around it (card add/remove/upgrade and exhibit add/remove). Hidden when everything is zero (e.g. mana events).

🔍 How to use the Run Finder tab

You can search runs by various conditions. Rough route choices are also visible.
For example: you want to see the deck of a Reimu-route run, you wonder how many runs had no AoE attack in Act 1, or you want to find users of a niche card, etc.

  • If you want to search for the final deck by specifying cards or exhibits, specify ACT4 / Lv4 (Lv5 or higher is also fine).
    If no ACT/Level is specified, all runs matching the conditions at any point are returned.
  • When ACT, level, cards, exhibits, etc. are specified, the hover on the runID shown in the play ID indicates whether it is a particular state (e.g. state act1 Lv 7), the final deck, or in which situation the item appeared.

    • * You can search for cards that were adopted mid-route but are not in the final result.

  • Pressing 🔗 Copy share link lets you share those search results.
  • The Player name field next to the deck size filters runs by that player (partial match, case-insensitive). Leave it empty for "no specification". Player names appearing in the logs appear as autocomplete candidates. It is included in the share link.

🗺️ Route conditions filter

The "Open route conditions filter ▼" (collapsed by default) narrows runs down by how the route was played. Pressing "+ Add condition row" adds a condition row (multiple rows must all be satisfied = AND).

  • A condition row = [Act (Total / Act1–3)] × [node type (Enemy / Elite / Event / Shop / Gap)] × [or more / or less / exactly] × [count].
    Example: "Total / Elite / or more / 2" = only runs that fought 2+ elites. "Act1 / Elite / or more / 1" = only runs that fought an elite at least once in Act 1.
  • The count is the number of nodes chosen (not win/loss). The ✕ button removes that condition.
  • Act 4 is excluded (it has a fixed route straight to the boss).
  • Included in the search history and share links (history labels show a notation like +R:A1X>=2).

🕰️ Search history comparison and IQR (Q1–Q3) display

After a search, the Search history comparison at the bottom of the page keeps the last 30 searches as a table and line charts of average life loss / turns per segment. Hovering a row or a chart point shows the condition's IQR (Q1–Q3).

  • Life sign: segment life is a net loss (heals included) — ‑ means damage-dominated, + means healing exceeded damage.
  • IQR band: the chart shows thin dashed Q1/Q3 lines with a gradient fill that is densest at the median (Q2). The Q2 line itself is drawn as a short dashed line.
  • Spread charts: line charts of each segment's Q3−Q1 width (how much the condition wobbles). Lower is better (smaller spread = more consistent results). Off by default — check "Spread (life) / Spread (turns)" in the "Charts to show" bar to show them.
  • Chart filtering & zoom: the 6 charts (2 segment + 2 spread (off by default) + 2 cumulative) are chosen with the "Charts to show" checkboxes (the bar is always visible). Click a chart to pop it up enlarged in a full-screen dialog (close with ✕ / ESC / backdrop click — hover highlighting and the IQR band stay interactive in the enlarged view). A single visible chart expands to full width automatically. "Reset" restores the defaults.
  • Computed only for conditions with 2 or more runs. Older history entries have no band — re-running the same condition adds it.

⚔️ "Battles Fought (by Enemy)" chart

The stacked bar chart shown below the search results summarizes, for the matched runs, how often each enemy was encountered and how many of those fights were hard battles.

  • Labels: 👹=Elite, ✨=Special Event, bosses show a character avatar (regular enemies have no prefix).
    Bars are sorted chronologically (Act → type → level), following the route order of the game.
  • Group background colors: the rows shaded with a soft background color by group — blue = early (lv0–5) / green = mid (lv5–10) / orange = late (lv10–15) / purple = elite / pink = boss / yellow = special event (the normal-enemy split uses the same lv bands as the Route selector, for Acts 1–3).
  • Hover details: hovering a bar (or the enemy name) shows encounter count, hard-fight rate, the hard-fight breakdown (life / spell / both), and box-whiskers of HP change (damage = − / heal = ＋) and turn count (Q0–Q4 = min/Q1/median/Q3/max values drawn inside the chart). The axis is shared within the same Act × enemy kind (a common min–max over that group, decided dynamically) — normal enemies are subdivided further by their appearance range within the Act (same bands as the Route selector: lv0–5 early / lv5–10 mid / lv10–15 late). When the search specifies a character, the (reference) hard-fight rate / breakdown and box-whiskers of that character's all runs are shown on the same axis in a lighter tone — a comparison against your own runs matching this search.
  • On narrow screens: long enemy names are wrapped and shown in a smaller font so the bars keep enough space (row spacing is widened accordingly).
  • Bar length: number of encounters by default. Use the button above the chart to switch to percentage mode (100% stacked — all bars equal length).
  • Dark segments = hard fights: 🩸Hard (HP) / ⚡️Hard (Spell card) / 🩸⚡️both, measured from the axis.
  • Text at the bar end = hard-fight rate (hard encounters / encounters).
    Hover a bar to see the detailed breakdown (encounters, hard fights, etc.).

* Values related to boss spell cards are estimates. Logs do not directly record spell card usage, so it is inferred from the power drop during the battle.
The threshold is relaxed for boss battles compared to regular ones, so actual usage may differ slightly.

🧮 Aggregation methods

1. Log collection & filtering

The latest log data is automatically collected from the public log repository (lbol logs). At that time, only logs suitable for analysis are selected, using the following criteria.

  • Only logs with a Lunatic7 true-end clear are used
  • Some mods are excluded (logs using mods that affect game balance are excluded)
  • To increase the sample size, only logs with vanilla cards and exhibits are extracted (if mod items are offered but not picked, the run is still reflected)
  • Suspicious logs are excluded (abnormal status changes, discontinuous data, etc.)

2. Per-player log sampling

If one player has too many logs, their play style would disproportionately affect the whole statistics. To prevent this, a cap is placed on the number of logs extracted per player.

Players below the cap are not restricted and all of their valid logs are used. Players over the cap are sampled via quantile-weighted sampling.

  1. Logs are split into 4 groups by recency (oldest / older / newer / newest, 25% each)
  2. More logs are drawn randomly from the newer groups, fewer from the older ones

3. How to read the numbers

  • Careful with rarely used cards: Cards with few fights show larger variation (few samples are smoothed toward the situation average, but treat them as a rough guide). Even when a number looks high, read it with the lack of track record in mind.
  • Some cards are not shown: Cards that do not meet the criteria (adoption rate, number of fights, etc.) are excluded from the display. Adoption Gap is also not shown for starting cards.
  • Sampling bias: The statistics are based on submitted logs, not a random sample. Treat them as reference values for grasping overall trends.

* The Stage-1 filter consists of runs that are not L7 clears, runs with mods, runs with inconsistent data, etc.
In the final check, NG = runs with abnormal numerical fluctuations, MOD = runs containing cards/exhibits that do not exist in vanilla.
Even if mods are installed, runs behaving nearly the same as vanilla are accepted.
