📌 Overview: what this report can do

Statistical data created from Lunatic7 clear runs of Touhou Kouyouya (LBOL).
It may help you get a rough grasp of the overall flow.

🗂️ Tabs

⚔️ Enemy AnalysisRough aggregates for various fights (in boss battles HP is restored after the fight, so HP numbers are not very accurate.)
❓ Event AnalysisVarious events listed in chronological order (details of choices are hard to capture, so please use your judgment)

🔍 Card Performance tab

You can see, at a rough glance, how much various cards contributed — both overall and situationally (Act 1 elites, etc.).
Further down the page there is also the Attention Score ranking, the Act 4 Performance Top 40, and the Tendency Top 40.
(The Attention Score ranking may be removed in the future.)

Medal
🥇 Gold / 🥈 Silver+ / 🥉 Bronze+ / No medal
The more impactful situations a card has, the better its medal. (Act 3 and boss fights are weighted.)
Adoption Gap
Close to 1 = the share picked at first offer and the share still in the final deck match (landing as expected).
Roughly Adoption Gap = Final Adoption ÷ pFirst, where pFirst = (offering rate) × (1st-pick rate at the first offering).
1.2x or more = adopted later than planned / 0.8–1.2x = adopted as planned / under 0.8x = a card that gets trimmed away over time
Cards landing above 1.2 come in two flavors: the "late-bloomer" type (low 1st Pick, adopted later) and the "staple" type (offered often and stacked in multiple copies). The gap alone cannot tell them apart, so also check 1st Pick and Avg. Copies.

* Adoption Gap does not function correctly for starting cards (they are never evaluated at first offer).

🔍 How to use the Run Finder tab

You can search runs by various conditions. Rough route choices are also visible.
For example: you want to see the deck of a Reimu-route run, you wonder how many runs had no AoE attack in Act 1, or you want to find users of a niche card, etc.

  • If you want to search for the final deck by specifying cards or exhibits, specify ACT4 / Lv4 (Lv5 or higher is also fine).
    If no ACT/Level is specified, all runs matching the conditions at any point are returned.
  • When ACT, level, cards, exhibits, etc. are specified, the hover on the runID shown in the play ID indicates whether it is a particular state (e.g. state act1 Lv 7), the final deck, or in which situation the item appeared.

    • * You can search for cards that were adopted mid-route but are not in the final result.

  • Pressing 🔗 Copy share link lets you share those search results.

⚔️ "Battles Fought (by Enemy)" chart

The stacked bar chart shown below the search results summarizes, for the matched runs, how often each enemy was encountered and how many of those fights were hard battles.

  • Labels: 👹=Elite, ✨=Special Event, bosses show a character avatar (regular enemies have no prefix).
    Bars are sorted chronologically (Act → type → level), following the route order of the game.
  • Bar length: number of encounters by default. Use the button above the chart to switch to percentage mode (100% stacked — all bars equal length).
  • Dark segments = hard fights: 🩸Hard (HP) / ⚡️Hard (Spell card) / 🩸⚡️both, measured from the axis.
  • Text at the bar end = hard-fight rate (hard encounters / encounters).
    Hover a bar to see the detailed breakdown (encounters, hard fights, etc.).

* Values related to boss spell cards are estimates. Logs do not directly record spell card usage, so it is inferred from the power drop during the battle.
The threshold is relaxed for boss battles compared to regular ones, so actual usage may differ slightly.

🧮 Aggregation methods

1. Log collection & filtering

The latest log data is automatically collected from the public log repository (lbol logs). At that time, only logs suitable for analysis are selected, using the following criteria.

  • Only logs with a Lunatic7 true-end clear are used
  • Some mods are excluded (logs using mods that affect game balance are excluded)
  • To increase the sample size, only logs with vanilla cards and exhibits are extracted (if mod items are offered but not picked, the run is still reflected)
  • Suspicious logs are excluded (abnormal status changes, discontinuous data, etc.)

2. Per-player log sampling

If one player has too many logs, their play style would disproportionately affect the whole statistics. To prevent this, a cap is placed on the number of logs extracted per player.

Players below the cap are not restricted and all of their valid logs are used. Players over the cap are sampled via quantile-weighted sampling.

  1. Logs are split into 4 groups by recency (oldest / older / newer / newest, 25% each)
  2. More logs are drawn randomly from the newer groups, fewer from the older ones

3. How to read the numbers

  • Careful with rarely used cards: Cards with few fights show larger variation (few samples are smoothed toward the situation average, but treat them as a rough guide). Even when a number looks high, read it with the lack of track record in mind.
  • Some cards are not shown: Cards that do not meet the criteria (adoption rate, number of fights, etc.) are excluded from the display. Adoption Gap is also not shown for starting cards.
  • Sampling bias: The statistics are based on submitted logs, not a random sample. Treat them as reference values for grasping overall trends.

* The Stage-1 filter consists of runs that are not L7 clears, runs with mods, runs with inconsistent data, etc.
In the final check, NG = runs with abnormal numerical fluctuations, MOD = runs containing cards/exhibits that do not exist in vanilla.
Even if mods are installed, runs behaving nearly the same as vanilla are accepted.
